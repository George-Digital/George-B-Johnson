# Output Scoring Rubric Template

**Use this rubric to score observable behavior, preserve reviewer evidence, and separate non-negotiable gates from preferences or aggregate scores.**

A score supports an operating decision for the stated use; it does not certify future performance or transfer a consequential decision from a qualified human to an AI system.

## Scale definitions

Choose one scale and define it before scoring.

### Pass

- **Level:** Pass
- **Definition:** The criterion is met with the required evidence.
- **Required operator action:** Continue to the next defined review or handoff.

### Revise

- **Level:** Revise
- **Definition:** The criterion is partly met or a noncritical issue needs correction.
- **Required operator action:** Revise, request information, or use the defined fallback.

### Fail gate

- **Level:** Fail gate
- **Definition:** A critical boundary, evidence, permission, routing, or decision rule is not met.
- **Required operator action:** Stop the case, contain it, and escalate as defined.
