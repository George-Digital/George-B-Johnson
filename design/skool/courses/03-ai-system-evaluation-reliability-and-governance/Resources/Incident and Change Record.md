# Incident and Change Record

**Use one record to connect an incident, failure, near miss, or stop condition to the decision, correction, safe test, approval, and follow-up that follow. Keep this record factual and link it to the Failure and Escalation Log; it does not replace required organizational reporting or qualified investigation.**

Do not copy personal, client, health, financial, credential, security, or other restricted case details into this template. Use a minimum-necessary safe summary and an approved secure reference. For high-impact decisions, AI output remains advisory; a qualified human with the appropriate authority makes the final decision.
