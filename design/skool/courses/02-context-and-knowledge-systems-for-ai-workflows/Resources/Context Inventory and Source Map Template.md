# Context Inventory and Source Map Template

Copy this template before you build the active Context Pack. Use one entry for each source or source category relevant to the bounded workflow. Keep restricted material in its approved location; this template should hold a safe reference, not unnecessary source contents.

## When to use

Use this template when starting a context-system build, when reviewing an existing source collection, and whenever a source is proposed for the active pack. It is designed to distinguish current authority from examples, candidates, and retired material.

## Required inputs

- The bounded workflow and intended user
- A source title, identifier, or approved reference
- The source owner or the role that can confirm ownership
- Known status, effective date, or last review date
- The source’s approved access boundary
- The question or output field the source may support

## Expected output

A completed inventory makes it possible to see which sources are active, what role they play, who owns them, how a claim can be traced, and why a source is excluded or pending.

## Practical example

For a field-service briefing workflow, the active inventory lists the approved safety checklist as the controlling rule source, the current scheduling guide as a secondary operating reference, and a sanitized completed brief as an example only. An undated technician note remains pending because its owner and effective scope are unknown. If the safety checklist is unavailable, the workflow stops and routes the case to the operations lead.

## Limitations and failure modes

An inventory does not prove that a source is correct, current, or approved. It can expose an owner or date gap, but the designated owner must resolve it. Do not treat availability, a familiar filename, or an AI-generated summary as authority. Do not place private records, credentials, or unnecessary personal information in the template.

## Human-review step

Ask the source owner or authorized reviewer to confirm the authority status, access boundary, effective status, and active-pack disposition for each source that will support important claims or decisions.
