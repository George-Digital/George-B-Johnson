## Applied prompt: prepare a cautious 90-day decision brief

**Use case:** You need to assemble approved operating evidence into a short decision brief for the people authorized to maintain, narrow, pause, or retire a system.

**Inputs:** Baseline; current scorecard; quality-review summaries; feedback themes; change and maintenance log; source and dependency status; ownership and review capacity; current scope; pause criteria.

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Prepare a concise 90-day operating decision brief from the supplied approved summaries. State the system’s purpose and current scope, then separate usage, quality, burden, feedback, source or dependency, ownership, and privacy or access findings. Preserve sample sizes, uncertainty, and comparison limitations. Evaluate only these options: maintain current scope, improve and retest, narrow scope, pause, replace, or retire. For each supported option, name the evidence, unresolved risk, required human approval, user communication, and manual fallback. Do not make a final decision, claim that a system is safe or effective, or override a qualified reviewer, source owner, privacy owner, or authorized decision-maker.

**Expected output:** A decision-ready summary with evidence, limitations, options, and human approval points.

**Failure mode:** The brief may conceal an important case-level concern inside an aggregate trend or lack the local authority context needed for retirement.

**Human review:** The operating owner checks factual accuracy. Authorized domain, source, privacy, access, technical, and decision owners review the portions within their responsibility and make the final operating decision.
