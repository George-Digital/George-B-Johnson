# Adoption & Continuous Improvement for AI Systems — Skool Upload Package

## Course name

Adoption & Continuous Improvement for AI Systems

## Paste-ready course description

Turn an active AI-assisted workflow into an owned operating process. Clarify ownership, onboard users, collect useful feedback, review changes, and decide when to maintain, pause, or retire the system. Built for operators with real users or operating evidence; it does not replace qualified judgment, privacy controls, or human review.

**Unicode character count:** 335

## Prerequisites and outcome

**Prerequisites:** Members bring an active AI-assisted system with real users or operating evidence. They can run and review the current workflow, identify its human-review boundary, and access the approved materials needed to operate it. No particular AI product, coding tool, API, or automation platform is required.

**Outcome:** Members finish with an owned operating process: a named system owner, onboarding and SOP materials, support and escalation routes, a bounded pilot record, a usage and quality scorecard, feedback and review routines, a change and maintenance log, and an evidence-based 30-, 60-, and 90-day decision to maintain, improve, pause, or retire the system.

## Verified Skool build workflow
