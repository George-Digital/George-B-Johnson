# Pilot and Go Live Checklist

**Use this checklist to run a controlled pilot and make a documented operating decision: continue pilot, launch within scope, revise, narrow, or pause.**

A completed checklist does not establish long-term reliability, permission for broad use, or suitability for sensitive or consequential work. Keep the pilot within its approved boundary.

## Pilot checks and output

- Confirm a complete common-path case, incomplete case, conflict or stale-source case, and stop-or-fallback case are available as safe, authorized, sanitized, or synthetic tests.
- Confirm the current version, trigger, validation, state labels, secure references, review gate, and output destination are documented.
- Confirm the operator can identify a duplicate, error, stalled review, and manual fallback route.
- Confirm the reviewer has the approved source reference and quality criteria.
- Record eligible cases, completed cases, pauses, manual routes, review returns, errors, operator time, reviewer time, wait time, recovery time, and direct or staff costs.
- Record quality, source, privacy, access, and approval gate results separately from efficiency measures.
- Record interventions, changed conditions, and evidence limits.
