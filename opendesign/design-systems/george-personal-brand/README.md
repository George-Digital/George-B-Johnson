# George B. Johnson personal-brand system

A reusable marketing design system for georgebjohnson.com. It positions George as a technical consultant specializing in AI-driven automation, with SEO engineering and digital operations as supporting competencies, while giving his businesses, tools, books, community, and service work a coherent home.

## Canonical positioning

**Technical Consultant — specializing in AI-driven automation.**

SEO engineering and digital operations support this primary positioning.

## Brand idea

**Useful automation. Engineered systems. Shared learning.**

The brand should feel like an experienced technical operator opening a well-kept field notebook: clear, warm, exact, and visibly constructed. Premium comes from restraint, typography, evidence, and detail—not luxury clichés or excessive animation.
