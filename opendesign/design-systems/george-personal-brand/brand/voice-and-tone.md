# Voice and tone

## Voice

George sounds like an experienced technical consultant explaining useful work to another capable person. The voice is direct, curious, grounded, and generous. It can be technical without becoming exclusionary.

## Point of view

Use first person on personal-brand pages. Use organization names when describing work owned by a company or community. Make relationships explicit instead of blending every initiative into one offer.

## Message hierarchy

1. George is a technical consultant specializing in AI-driven automation.
2. SEO engineering and digital operations are supporting competencies.
3. The work is visible through George Digital, AtsKaleSEO, Builders Lab, books, and community consulting.
4. Readers can explore the work, learn from field notes, or join the community.
5. Proof should come from linked projects, dates, published material, and approved results.

## Style rules

- Sentence case for headings, buttons, and navigation.
- Short paragraphs; one idea per paragraph.
- Use concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Use “SEO” and “AI” after the full concept is clear from context.
- Prefer “I build,” “I test,” and “I document” over “I help brands unlock.”
- Use the Oxford comma.
- Spell out one through nine in prose; use numerals for technical values and dates.
- No emoji in core website copy.
- Avoid exclamation marks except in quoted material.

## CTA language

Primary actions should describe the destination:

- Explore the work
- Read the field notes
- See the projects
- Join Builders Lab
- View the book
- Discuss community consulting

Avoid generic labels such as “Learn more,” “Get started,” and “Click here.”

## Claims policy

Do not publish credentials, client counts, rankings, revenue, ROI, testimonials, or performance statistics without an approved source. Existing biographical copy may guide research but is not automatically approved proof.

Avoid or retire unsupported phrases such as:

- thought leader
- pioneering force
- top brands
- outstanding returns
- proven success
- guaranteed growth
