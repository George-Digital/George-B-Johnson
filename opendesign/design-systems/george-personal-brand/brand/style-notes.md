# Visual foundations

## Art direction

**Premium field system:** editorial typography laid over the visible logic of an engineering notebook. Warm paper creates approachability; graphite creates authority; bronze connects to the existing GBJ mark; teal appears only as a secondary system signal.

## Color

- Paper is the default reading surface.
- Graphite anchors navigation, hero framing, and major calls to action.
- Bronze indicates the active path, key links, and selected work.
- Teal is reserved for system status, focus, and occasional technical metadata.
- Never use bronze and teal evenly. Bronze is dominant.

## Typography

- Instrument Serif: memorable statements and section headlines.
- Manrope: paragraphs, navigation, controls, and supporting headings.
- IBM Plex Mono: labels, timestamps, indexes, coordinates, and status text.
- Keep display headlines short enough to preserve their silhouette.

## Layout

- Use a twelve-column desktop grid and a four-column mobile grid.
- Let rules and column boundaries remain visible in selected sections.
- Favor asymmetric editorial compositions over centered SaaS stacks.
- Keep body-copy measures between 52 and 68 characters.
- Use generous section spacing, then dense information inside project indexes.

## Components

- Buttons: square or lightly rounded with visible text and arrow movement.
- Cards: bordered editorial panels; avoid floating rounded-card collections.
- Project index: numbered rows with role, purpose, and destination.
- Status labels: mono, uppercase, small, and never used as decoration alone.
- Portrait: honest crop, no AI beautification, framed with project metadata rather than decorative effects.

## Backgrounds

- Warm paper with a low-contrast grid.
- Graphite with thin warm-white rules.
- Optional paper grain at very low opacity when a real texture is available.
- No default gradient mesh, glass panel, or particle field.

## Motion

- Signature motion: a bronze signal line traverses the hero grid once.
- Supporting motion: project rows reveal their accent rule and links shift by 4–6px.
- Scroll reveals should be subtle and should never hide crawlable content by default.
- Motion durations use the canonical tokens and stop under reduced-motion preferences.
- No artificial page-loading delay.

## Interaction states

- Hover changes underline/rule length and small positional offsets.
- Press reduces scale no more than 1–2%.
- Focus uses a dual paper-and-graphite ring so it remains visible on dark, light, and bronze surfaces.
- Mobile targets are at least 44px.

## Imagery

Use real portraits, book covers, product interfaces, diagrams, and working artifacts. AI-generated imagery is acceptable only for clearly illustrative atmospheres—not fabricated proof, events, clients, products, or credentials.
