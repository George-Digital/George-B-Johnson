# George B. Johnson personal-brand system

Use this system for georgebjohnson.com marketing pages and related personal-brand assets.

## Core direction

- Present George as a technical consultant specializing in AI-driven automation.
- Treat SEO engineering and digital operations as supporting competencies.
- Use an entity-led hierarchy: George first, then the connected systems and organizations.
- Create a premium impression through editorial typography, exact spacing, evidence, and restraint.
- Pair warm paper surfaces with graphite fields and bronze signal accents.
- Use technical grids, rules, coordinates, and signal paths sparingly as structural motifs.

## Typography

- Display: Instrument Serif for short, high-impact statements.
- Body/UI: Manrope for clarity and modern technical credibility.
- Metadata: IBM Plex Mono for labels, coordinates, dates, and system states.
- Do not set long body passages in the display serif.
- Keep sentence case except short navigation and system labels.

## Copy

- Prefer first person and active verbs: build, engineer, test, document, teach.
- Connect claims to visible projects or approved evidence.
- Avoid thought-leader language, inflated superlatives, guarantees, and vague growth promises.
- Explain the relationship among George Digital, AtsKaleSEO, Builders Lab, books, and consulting.
- Keep the primary homepage promise broader than any one company or product.

## Components and layout

- Use editorial split layouts, asymmetric project indexes, and visible rules.
- Keep cards mostly square or subtly rounded; avoid pill-card grids.
- Reserve pills for status and taxonomy.
- Use one dominant motion idea per page plus simple state transitions.
- All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain 44px mobile targets, visible focus states, semantic headings, and static crawlable copy.

## Avoid

- Purple/blue AI gradients
- Glassmorphism as a default surface
- Artificial loading delays
- Generic three-card SaaS feature sections
- Decorative metrics or client logos without source evidence
- Excessive typewriter, particle, parallax, or scroll-jacking effects
- Presenting 21st.dev components without adapting them to this system
